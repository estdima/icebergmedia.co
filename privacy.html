Privacy policy

This page explains what we do with the details you send us through icebergmedia.co. In short: we use them to reply to you, we keep them safe, and we never sell them.

Who we are

Iceberg Media makes ads and runs ad campaigns for ecommerce brands. It is run by its founder, Dimitrius, who is responsible for your personal data. For anything about your data, email dimitrius@icebergmedia.co.

What we collect

  • When you fill in the contact form: your name, your company website, your email address and anything you write in Notes. We also save the date, the page you sent it from and how you found us, for example the campaign name in the link you clicked or the site that linked to us.
  • When you email us: your email address and what your message contains.
  • When you visit the site: our hosting provider handles technical data such as your IP address, browser and the pages you open, so it can deliver the site and keep it secure. We don't use this to identify you.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels or any other tracking. Our fonts and images are served from our own site, so opening a page doesn't send your details to anyone else.

How we use your details

  • To reply to your enquiry and set up a call.
  • To send you one automatic email confirming we've received your details.
  • To prepare for the call, for example by looking at your website and the ads you already run publicly.
  • To keep a record of enquiries, and to learn which of our own ads and links bring them.

We won't add you to a newsletter or mailing list, and we never sell your details.

Our legal basis

Under the UK and EU General Data Protection Regulation, we use your details because you asked us to take steps before a possible contract (replying and arranging a call), and because we have a legitimate interest in answering business enquiries and running our business.

Who we share them with

Only with the services we use to run Iceberg Media, which handle your details on our behalf:

  • Google (Google Workspace), for our email and the spreadsheet where enquiries are stored.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website.

We would only share your details with anyone else if the law required it.

Transfers outside the UK and EU

Google and GitHub may process data in the United States and other countries. They protect it with the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission and the UK, and with the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where it applies.

How long we keep them

If an enquiry doesn't lead to working together, we delete it within 24 months of our last contact. If we do work together, we keep what we need for the contract and for tax and accounting rules.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your details, to limit or stop how we use them, or to send them to you in a common format. Email dimitrius@icebergmedia.co and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to a data protection authority, such as the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK (ico.org.uk) or the authority in your EU country.

If you live in a US state with a privacy law, such as California, you have similar rights to know, correct and delete your details. We don't sell or share personal information for advertising.

Keeping your details safe

We use established providers, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and limit access to the people who need it. No method is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care.

Children

Our services are for businesses and aren't directed at anyone under 16.

Changes

If anything changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
